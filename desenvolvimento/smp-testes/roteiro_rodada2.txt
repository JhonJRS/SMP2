## ArcadiaGuard: zona criada por coordenadas (sem WorldEdit)
> forceload add -16 -16 16 16
> setblock 0 200 0 minecraft:stone
> setblock 12 200 12 minecraft:stone
> smpteste quebrar 12 200 12 => QUEBROU
> ag help
> ag zone add teste -5 190 -5 5 210 5 => teste
> ag zone list => teste
> ag zone info teste
> smpteste quebrar 0 200 0 => PROTEGIDO
> setblock 3 200 3 minecraft:stone
> ag flag teste block_break allow
> smpteste quebrar 3 200 3
> ag zone remove teste => teste
