# ArcadiaGuard: zona por coordenadas + flags (sem WorldEdit)

## Zona e proteção de blocos
> forceload add -16 -16 16 16
> setblock 0 200 0 minecraft:stone
> setblock 2 200 2 minecraft:stone
> setblock 12 200 12 minecraft:stone
> ag zone add teste -5 190 -5 5 210 5 => Zone created
> ag zone flag teste block-break deny => block-break
> ag zone flag teste block-place deny => block-place
> ag zone flag teste pvp deny => pvp
> ag zone flag teste irons-spell-cast deny => irons-spell-cast
> ag zone info teste
> smpteste quebrar 0 200 0 => PROTEGIDO
> smpteste quebrar 12 200 12 => QUEBROU
> ag zone flag teste block-break allow => block-break
> smpteste quebrar 2 200 2 => QUEBROU
> ag log teste
> ag zone remove teste => removed
